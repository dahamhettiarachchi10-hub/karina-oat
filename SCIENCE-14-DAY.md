# 14-day OAT science plan

The starter release adds 129 original practice questions (36 General Chemistry, 42 Organic Chemistry, 51 Physics), 43 topics and 13 modules. Home opens the next unchecked study day. Day 1 has six questions in each of the four sciences. Days 10 and 12 are timed practice: 100 Natural Sciences questions in 90 minutes (40 Biology, 30 General Chemistry, 30 Organic Chemistry), and 40 Physics questions in 50 minutes. Scores are practice accuracy. The main prep program remains the source for full-length mocks and the unbuilt Reading Comprehension and Quantitative Reasoning sections.

| Source | Modules | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| General Chemistry Equation Sheet pp. 1–4 | Foundations, Gases & Solutions, Equilibrium, Energy | Added conceptual guidance on atomic trends, bonding, IMFs and measurement where the sheet is sparse. |
| Organic Chemistry Lessons ch. 1–14 (PDF pp. 2–65) | Structure, Analysis, Reactions, Carbonyls | Prioritized mechanisms, stereochemistry, spectroscopy, and product selection. Worked examples are original. |
| Physics Equation Sheet pp. 1–17 | Motion, Energy, Fluids/Heat/Waves, Electric, Optics | Always state the model, unit choices and signs. |
| Quantitative Reasoning Formula Sheet pp. 1–2 | Math Reference | Science support and common math tools. |
| ADA 2026 Candidate Guide, Scope of the Examination | Science timing and content emphasis | Organic Chemistry follows the post-June 2026 headings. |

The new PDFs are references for original lessons and questions; they are not bundled. The existing Biology sourcebook, previous Biology questions, recurring Biology bank manifest, and 24-chapter release rules are retained. Content is prioritized for short review rather than presented as an exhaustive course. Existing browser progress stays on version 1; new day-completion and first-try accuracy fields are optional on old exports. Correct guesses can be flagged; a mistake round pulls untried items from the same topic or skill where available.
