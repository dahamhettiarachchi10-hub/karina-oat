# Biology source coverage

The supplied Drive folder contains two identical copies of a 136-page DAT Bootcamp biology PDF. The site follows all 24 instructional chapters/subchapters (pages 4–136); pages 1–3 are introductory material and contents. This source is biology only, not the entire multi-subject OAT syllabus.

Guides and questions are original adaptations of the source concepts, not a verbatim reproduction. The complete PDF is bundled at source/bio-notes.pdf, with chapter and topic links to the relevant pages, preserving every original figure and example. Source simplifications are qualified where needed, including ATP yield, Hardy–Weinberg assumptions, vestigial structures, and life-history categories.
